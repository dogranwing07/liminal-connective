WHY LIMINAL
CONNECTIVE.

A name for the threshold between what already exists and what comes next. The space between is not empty. It is where new possibilities begin.

LIMINAL

LIMINAL describes the transitional space between one state and the next. Japan and Vietnam. Culture and culture. People and people. Brand and market. The present and the future.

We are interested in the place where definitions begin to loosen. The place where something familiar can be seen again from a different angle.

CONNECTIVE

CONNECTIVE is more than connection. For us it contains three movements: CONNECT, COLLECTIVE and CREATIVE.

Connect different worlds. Bring different people and capabilities together. Then reconnect what already exists into a form that has not existed before.

CONNECT PEOPLE.
COLLECT IDEAS.
CREATE THE FUTURE.

LIMINAL CONNECTIVE is therefore not a description of one business category. It is a position: standing at the boundary, connecting people, ideas and possibilities, and building the structure for what comes next.
